DATE AI — ACCOUNT & BILLING

Payment Methods
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal for Starter and Pro plans.
- Enterprise Suite customers may also pay via bank transfer or company purchase order, arranged through your dedicated account manager.

Billing Cycles
- Starter Plan: billed monthly on the date of initial signup, or annually if the annual discount option was selected.
- Pro License: billed annually only, on the anniversary of the original purchase date.
- Enterprise Suite: billed annually or biennially per the signed service agreement; biennial billing includes a 15% discount.

Invoices
- Invoices are automatically emailed to the account's billing contact after each successful charge.
- Past invoices can be downloaded anytime from Settings > Billing > Invoice History.
- Enterprise customers requiring custom invoice formatting (PO numbers, tax IDs, etc.) should contact their account manager.

Failed Payments
- If a payment fails, we retry automatically after 3 days and again after 7 days.
- Accounts are suspended (read-only access) after 2 failed retry attempts, and fully deactivated after 14 days of non-payment.
- To resolve a failed payment, update your payment method under Settings > Billing > Payment Methods, then click "Retry Payment Now."

Multi-User Seats
- Starter Plan includes 1 seat; additional seats are not available on this tier.
- Pro License includes 5 seats; additional seats can be added at $15/seat/month.
- Enterprise Suite includes unlimited seats as part of the base contract.
- Seat management is available under Settings > Team > Manage Seats (admin access required).

Account Deletion
- Account deletion is permanent and removes all associated data, including knowledge base content and conversation history, after a 30-day recovery grace period.
- To request account deletion, go to Settings > Account > Delete Account, or contact support directly for assistance.
